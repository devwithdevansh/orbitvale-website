Website structure & motion map

A dark, high-motion site built from 31 ready-made animated components already in the repo. This document is the blueprint: every section on every page, which component fills it, and how the scroll story is sequenced — before a single line of the real build goes in.

01 — Design system

Base
#0B0B0D
Near-black. Every section sits on this; nothing is pure #000, so glows read as light rather than holes.
Ink
#F2F2F4
Off-white for text — matches the logo's white wordmark without the harshness of pure white.
Accent — primary
Mint / cyan #7FF3D0
The "orbit" light. Used sparingly: single planet dot, active nav, one glowing CTA.
Accent — secondary
Warm #FFB86B
Optional partner tone for the accretion / black-hole sections only, so those moments feel hot.
Typography
Stencil display + geometric sans
Headlines use a wide-tracked stencil/geometric face echoing the ORBITVALE wordmark (tracking ~0.3em on the brand, less on body). Body copy in a clean geometric sans at 15–17px, 1.7 line-height. Mono used for labels, indices and the "01 / 06" counters.
Motion rules
Scroll-driven, one hero per page
Exactly one full-bleed 3D moment per viewport — never two competing shaders. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, and all heavy canvases pause when off-screen.
