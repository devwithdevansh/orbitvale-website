PART I: EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PHILOSOPHICAL COMPARISON
================================================================================

Why do these two sites win Awwwards Site of the Day / Site of the Month?

1. HALDEN (The Masterclass in Editorial Restraint & Tactile 2.5D):
   - Design Philosophy: "Useful rather than loud." Halden relies on high-end Swiss
     editorial typography, spacious layouts, subtle spring physics, and physical
     card stacking.
   - Key Strength: Restraint. It never uses visual effects that scream for
     attention; instead, every interaction feels like handling fine, heavy paper,
     machined cards, and precision mechanical clocks.
   - Visual Hallmarks:
     * Custom magnetic pill cursor that morphs into contextual action badges ("View", "Read").
     * Rolling odometer slot-machine digit counters (CSS translateY on number strips).
     * 3D Card Fan Hero: Multiple project cards that fan out with spring physics on pointer move.
     * Inline video capsules nested directly inside typography sentences.
     * Sticky stacking card deck where cards tilt and stack on top of each other as you scroll.
     * Interactive availability month bar (visual capacity schedule).
     * Giant dark brand footer with live world clocks (Oslo & Lisbon).

2. LUSION (The Pinnacle of Immersive 3D WebGL & Dimensional Spatial Storytelling):
   - Design Philosophy: "One continuous journey." Lusion does not feel like a website;
     it feels like flying through a high-budget 3D animated film with real-time WebGL.
   - Key Strength: Single continuous 3D camera flight through space, objects, and dimensions.
   - Visual Hallmarks:
     * Rigid-body 3D physics cluster in the hero (interlocking jacks you can swat and bounce).
     * 3D glowing spline ribbons that weave through HTML text like roller-coaster rails.
     * Zero-G floating astronaut with real-time limb drift and pointer tracking.
     * Flying through 3D typography in spatial depth with depth of field blur.
     * Chromatic aberration lens flare rings (optical dispersion, red/green/blue splitting).
     * Infinite hypercube mirror tunnel with glitching neon circuitry.
     * Blinding white-pink crystalline wormhole / singularity explosion.
     * Dimension breakout: astronaut shattering 3D glass shards directly toward the user camera.
     * Interactive physics sticker playground.

PART IV: THE UNDERLYING TECH STACK & ENGINE ANATOMY
================================================================================

What makes these engines run at 60–120 FPS?

1. RENDERING & PIPELINE:
   - Halden:
     * Next.js / Webflow clean DOM structure.
     * Lenis (Studio Freight) for smooth momentum scrolling.
     * CSS 3D transforms (`perspective: 1200px`, `transform-style: preserve-3d`).
     * GSAP / Framer Motion for spring physics and timeline sequencing.
     * SVG path drawing for timeline connectors.
   - Lusion:
     * React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber) + Three.js.
     * Custom GLSL Shaders (vertex shaders for ribbon deformation, fragment shaders for
       chromatic aberration, volumetric lighting, and fresnel reflections).
     * Postprocessing pipeline: UnrealBloomPass, ChromaticAberrationEffect, NoiseEffect,
       DepthOfFieldEffect.
     * Physics Engine: Rapier.js / Havok / Cannon-es for real-time rigid-body collisions
       (jacks cluster and shattered glass particles).
     * SkinnedMesh with skeletal animation for the astronaut.

2. CAMERA & SCROLL SYNCHRONIZATION:
   - Halden: Scroll triggers map `window.scrollY / scrollHeight` to discrete phase ranges.
   - Lusion: A master scroll timeline (similar to GSAP ScrollTrigger) controls the camera's
     Z-position and curve orientation along a 3D spline. Scrolling does not move HTML
     elements; scrolling drives the 3D camera along a CatmullRomCurve3 path in WebGL!

3. TYPOGRAPHY & BRAND TOKENS:
   - Halden:
     * Primary font: Crisp geometric sans-serif (similar to Syne or Neue Montreal).
     * Accent font: High-contrast calligraphic italic serif for emotional keywords ("Work", "Stop For", "Clients").
     * Palette: Off-white slate (#f4f6fa), rich black (#0a0a0c), electric blue (#0055ff).
   - Lusion:
     * Display font: Heavyweight grotesque sans-serif with tight letter-spacing.
     * Monospace: Technical telemetry font for preloader and micro-labels.
     * Palette: Void black (#000000), chalk white (#ffffff), hyper-blue (#0033ff), crimson red (#ff2233).
